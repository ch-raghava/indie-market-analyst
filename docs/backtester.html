Backtester

A pure pandas and numpy engine built to simulate Indian market conditions with surgical precision. No hallucinations, just math.

01

The Indian Cost Model

Most backtesters assume zero friction or use a flat percentage. We model the complex reality of Indian transaction costs, including:

  • · STT (Securities Transaction Tax)
  • · Stamp Duty
  • · Exchange Transaction Charges
  • · SEBI Turnover Fees
  • · GST (18% on brokerage & charges)
  • · Zerodha-style flat brokerage
02

Performance Metrics

The engine calculates risk-adjusted returns beyond just CAGR. It provides:

  • Sharpe Ratio & Sortino Ratio
  • Maximum Drawdown (MDD)
  • Calmar Ratio
  • Volatility (Annualized)
  • Win Rate & Expectancy
Usage

Running a Simulation

The backtester can be used as a standalone library or via the agent's tools.

from backtest.engines.equity_engine import BacktestEngine

engine = BacktestEngine(initial_capital=100000)
results = engine.run(strategy_signals, price_data)

print(f"Sharpe Ratio: {results.sharpe_ratio}")
